Since it's tied to pornography specifically, that's really the root of the tree, and the "in public" gaze issue is often a downstream symptom of that deeper habit (increased desensitization/craving makes casual glances harder to control too). So the plan below treats the porn habit as priority #1, with gaze-lowering in daily life as the parallel practice.

You have about 9 weeks until October. That's enough time to build real momentum if you're consistent — not to become "perfect," but to be in a fundamentally different, healthier place by your wedding.

## The Plan

### 1. Cut off access (Week 1 — do this today)
This isn't optional groundwork, it's the foundation. Willpower alone rarely beats an addictive habit with zero friction.
- Install a filter/accountability app on every device (Covenant Eyes, Qustodio, or built-in ones like Apple Screen Time / Google Family Link with a **trusted person** holding the password — not yourself).
- Delete/restrict any app or browser habit that's a direct gateway (private browsing, specific apps, bookmarks).
- If social media is a trigger, mute/unfollow accounts that push content your gaze struggles with, and set a screen time limit on Instagram/TikTok/YouTube specifically.

### 2. Get one accountability person (this week)
Someone you check in with weekly — a close brother, an imam, or a support structure. Isolation is where relapse thrives. This doesn't need to be dramatic; even a short weekly "how did this week go" text to one trusted person cuts relapse rates significantly.

### 3. Understand your triggers (ongoing, start now)
For the next 2 weeks, briefly note (phone notes app is fine) *when* urges hit: boredom, late night, stress, being alone, specific apps, certain times of day. You're not analyzing deeply — just spotting patterns so you can disrupt them (e.g., if it's always late at night alone, that's when you put the phone in another room).

### 4. Daily spiritual anchors (start now, keep for life)
These aren't separate from the "practical" plan — they're what sustains it long-term:
- **Fajr consistently.** This single habit correlates with everything else falling into place for most people.
- A short daily dua for protection of the gaze/heart (even something simple and sincere, in your own words, before you open your phone).
- Regular Qur'an recitation/listening — even 10 minutes — especially at trigger times (e.g., late evening).
- Fasting Mondays/Thursdays if you're able — you mentioned you've used this before, so lean back into it as a monthly rhythm, not just crisis mode.

### 5. Public gaze — the "first glance, not second" rule
You're not expected to walk around staring at the floor. The practical standard is: the first involuntary glance isn't sinful, the deliberate second look is what you're training against.
- When you catch a lingering look, immediately redirect — look down, look away, or engage your mind (a dhikr, a thought, anything to break the fixation loop).
- Treat it like a rep in the gym: every redirected glance is a small win, not a referendum on your character.

### 6. Replace the void, don't just remove
Habits like this often fill boredom, stress, or loneliness. Fill that space deliberately: exercise, a hobby, reading, more time with people, more time with brothers at the masjid. An empty schedule with just "don't do X" rarely holds.

### 7. Handle relapse without spiraling
If you slip, the two things that actually matter: (1) don't let shame push you into secrecy or hopelessness — Allah's mercy is bigger than the slip, and (2) get back to the routine within the day, not "starting Monday." Momentum breaks from despair, not from single mistakes.

### 8. As October approaches
Two things worth doing in the final weeks: talk to your future spouse (at whatever level is appropriate pre-marriage) about wanting a clean start together, and understand that marriage itself is part of Allah's prescribed solution here (ﷺ told the young men to marry, and if unable, to fast) — so lean into anticipation of that as motivation, not just restraint.
