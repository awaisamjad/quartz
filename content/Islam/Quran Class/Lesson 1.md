# Surah Baqarah

- Described as the 'peak' - something akin to a peak of a mountain, very grand, high, grandiore, majesty of something
  - This shows it should be given great importance
- Surah Baqarah is the peak of the Quran

> Abu Huraira reported: The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said,
> “Everything has a summit, and the summit of the Quran is Surat al-Baqarah. It contains a verse which is the chief of the Quran, the verse of the Throne.”
> Source: Sunan al-Tirmidhī 2878
> Grade: Hasan li ghayrihi (fair due to external evidence) according to Al-Albani
> عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ لِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ سَنَامٌ وَإِنَّ سَنَامَ القُرْآنِ سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ وَفِيهَا آيَةٌ هِيَ سَيِّدَةُ آيِ القُرْآنِ هِيَ آيَةُ الكُرْسِيِّ
> 2878 سنن الترمذي أبواب فضائل القرآن باب ما جاء في فضل سورة البقرة وآية الكرسي
> 1461 المحدث الألباني خلاصة حكم المحدث حسن لغيره في صحيح الترغيب

- described as a 'tent' - a protective covering
- Baqarah i.e the cow is rich in its meat
- All the names given to the surah points to the magnificence of the surah

## Virtues of the last 2 verses

- Will suffice the person if they recite them at night
- These verses were revealed directly to the prophet from Allah in 'Isra wal Miraj', not through Jibreel
- Why were these 2 grand verses released at the **end** of the surah
  - The surah itself is so grand so right at the end a fitting end is brought by these 2 verses
  - The story of the Children of Israel and their failure and transgression, how we are now responsible like they were, we may feel its too much to bear and the verses comfort us

## Virtue of the greatest of verse Ayat al-Kursi

- When someone knew Al Baqarah and Al Imran they were held in high regard among the companions

## (Verses 3 - 5)


  - Hereafter

## Attributes of the Disbelievers (Verses 6 - 7)

```
إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ سَوَآءٌ عَلَيْهِمْ ءَأَنذَرْتَهُمْ أَمْ لَمْ تُنذِرْهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ ٦

```
