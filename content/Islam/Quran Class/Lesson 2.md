```
يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ رَبَّكُمُ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَكُمْ وَٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ ٢١
O mankind! Worship your Lord (Allâh), Who created you and those who were before you so that you may become Al-Muttaqûn (the pious - See V.2:2).
```

- Why the word 'Mankind'
  - Mankind is of 3 groups: Believers, Hypocrites, and Disbelievers
  - We were told of this is in the previous verses
  - The message to 'Worship your Lord' is so great that all should recieve this message
- Why the word (رَبَّكُمُ) 'Lord' and not 'Allah'
  - The word also has connotations of nurturer, provider, taking care. It makes it more personal
  - In a persons fitrah one has the desire to be taken care of?

```
ٱلَّذِى جَعَلَ لَكُمُ ٱلْأَرْضَ فِرَٰشًا وَٱلسَّمَآءَ بِنَآءً وَأَنزَلَ مِنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ مَآءً فَأَخْرَجَ بِهِۦ مِنَ ٱلثَّمَرَٰتِ رِزْقًا لَّكُمْ ۖ فَلَا تَجْعَلُوا۟ لِلَّهِ أَندَادًا وَأَنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ ٢٢
Who has made the earth a resting place for you, and the sky as a canopy, and sent down water (rain) from the sky and brought forth therewith fruits as a provision for you. Then do not set up rivals unto Allâh (in worship) while you know (that He Alone has the right to be worshipped).
```

- Allah uses his attributes that can't be attributed to anyone else to show that He is the only that can be worshipped
