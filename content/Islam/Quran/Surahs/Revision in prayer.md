2. Distribute your daily recitation across multiple prayer times as follows:
   - Duha (4 Rakah): Recite **Mulk**, **Qalam**, **Haqqah**, **Ma'arij**, **Nuh**, **Jinn**
   - Zuhr (6 Rakah): Recite the remaining half of Juz 29 and all of Juz 30.
   - Asr (6 Rakah): Review the previous day's recitation, focusing on any verses that might have been forgotten or need improvement. You can also revise a small portion from the current Juz you are learning.
   - Maghreb (5 Rakah) and Isha (6 Rakah): Use these prayers for regular worship and not for Quran recitation to ensure you have enough time and focus during the other prayers.

**Proposed 4-Day Plan (2 Juz Total)**

I’m going to organize this assuming you want to dedicate roughly equal time to 
each, recognizing Juz An-Nas is longer. I’ll include suggested prayer times, but 
adjust based on *your* schedule and preference.

**Day 1: Focus on Mulk (Partial)**

* **Prayer:** Zuhr (6 Rak'ah)
* **Section:** Juz' Mulk, verses 1-20  (approx. 20 minutes)
* **Action:** Recite the verses slowly and clearly.  Focus on pronunciation and

needed.

**Day 2: Focus on Mulk (Completion) & Nas (Partial)**

* **Prayer:** Asr (6 Rak'ah)
* **Section:**
    * Juz' Mulk, verses 21-48 (approx. 10 minutes)
    * Juz' An-Nas, verses 1-30 (approx. 15 minutes)
* **Action:**  Same focus as Day 1 - slow, clear, and understanding.

**Day 3: Focus on Nas (Major Section)**

* **Prayer:** Maghrib (5 Rak'ah)
* **Section:** Juz' An-Nas, verses 31-70 (approx. 25 minutes)
* **Action:** This is a longer section. Break it down mentally into smaller 
chunks if needed.  Focus on the flow of the verses.

**Day 4: Focus on Nas (Completion & Review)**

* **Prayer:** Isha (6 Rak'ah)
* **Section:**
    * Juz' An-Nas, verses 71-114 (approx. 20 minutes)
    * **Brief Review:** Quickly recite verses 1-10 from Juz’ Mulk, and 1-10 from 
Juz’ An-Nas. (5 minutes)
* **Action:**  Complete the remaining verses.  The brief review reinforces the 
material.

**Important Considerations & Tips for Success:**

* **Warm-Up:** Before starting each recitation, do a quick review of the verses 
you’ll be reciting.  Just quickly glance through them, remembering the key words 
and phrases.
* **Pronunciation:**  Don't rush to understand the meaning if your pronunciation 
is off. Focus on getting the Arabic correct first. Use a reliable recitation 
(like Mishary Rashid Al-Afasy) as a reference.
* **Understanding (Tafsir):**  Even a very basic understanding of the meaning can 
greatly enhance your connection to the verses.  Look up brief tafsir notes online 
after your recitation.
* **Breaks:** Don’t hesitate to take short breaks within a prayer if needed.  
It's better to pause and refocus than to rush and make mistakes.
* **Record Yourself:**  This can be a powerful tool for identifying areas for 
improvement in pronunciation and recitation.
* **Gradual Increase:**  As you become more comfortable, you can gradually 
increase the number of verses you recite in each prayer, or add another shorter 
prayer time.
* **Duha Prayer:** The Duha prayer is ideal for shorter reviews or reinforcing 
specific verses. You can use this to review a portion of the previous day's 
recitation.
* **Be Patient:** Memorization and revision take time and effort. Don’t get 
discouraged if you don't see results immediately.  Consistency is key.
* **Intention (Niyyah):**  Remember your intention is to draw closer to Allah 
through the Quran.  That intention itself is a great reward.
