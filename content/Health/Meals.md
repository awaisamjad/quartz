### 07:30 — Pre-Workout

#### Current Recipe

- 2 slices white toast + Nutella (~30g)
- 1 piece of whole fruit (Orange, Grapes, or Apple)
- 200 ml Fruit juice (Orange, Pineapple, or Cranberry)

#### ⚡ Nutrition & Macro Recalculation

- **Calories:** ~470 kcal
- **Protein:** ~6g
- **Carbs:** ~88g (high glycemic index)
- **Fat:** ~10.5g
- _Analysis:_ This is a high-sugar, low-fiber meal. While the rapid-acting carbohydrates provide immediate energy, the lack of complex carbs can trigger an insulin spike followed by a mid-workout crash (reactive hypoglycemia).

#### 💡 Suggested Upgrade

- Swap white toast for **sourdough or wholemeal toast** (complex carbs for sustained energy).
- Replace Nutella with **almond or peanut butter** + a sliced **banana** and a light drizzle of honey. This gives you natural simple sugars alongside healthy fats and fiber to slow down digestion just enough to prevent a crash.
- Eat the whole fruit and swap the juice for water or green tea to reduce excess sugar intake.
